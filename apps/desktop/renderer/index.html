Definitions are a signed set — they apply as a whole and cannot be turned off one family at a time in v1. The escape hatch is below: one command can be allowed through the denylist (for example, your own deploy script).

Notifications

      
When the wall blocks something, FinePrint also tells you through the operating system — macOS Notification Center or Windows Action Center — so a block is visible even when the app is behind something else.
